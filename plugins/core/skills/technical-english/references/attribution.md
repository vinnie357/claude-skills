# Attribution

`/core:technical-english` draws on two external sources for its two registers. Neither source's text is reproduced here — each principle is paraphrased and attributed, per the rules below.

## Strict mode — informed by ASD-STE100

The strict rule set is informed by the shape of ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English), a controlled-language standard for aerospace and defense maintenance documentation. ASD-STE100 is fully owned by ASD (Aerospace, Security and Defence Industries Association of Europe), Brussels. The ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group (STEMG) develops, publishes, and maintains it, and the specification site carries a STEMG copyright notice reserving all rights. No reproduction license is published.

Facts about the standard, safe to state. All were read from https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html on 2026-08-27:

- Current edition: Issue 9, January 2025.
- 53 writing rules across 9 sections, plus a controlled dictionary.
- Roughly 900 approved and 1,200 unapproved words in that dictionary.
- Maintained by the ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group, Brussels.
- Available free on request from the maintenance group.

This skill does not reproduce ASD-STE100's rule text, its numbered rule list, or any part of its dictionary. It paraphrases rule *categories* into original wording and builds its own repo-specific examples and word-choice table.

**Never claim conformance.** This skill and its output are "informed by" or "paraphrased from" the principles behind ASD-STE100. They are never "compliant" or "conformant" with the standard — that claim requires certification this project has not sought.

## Flavored mode — informed by Zinsser's four principles

The flavored register restates four principles from William Zinsser's *On Writing Well* — Clarity, Simplicity, Brevity, and Humanity — in this repo's own words. The restatement is original text; the four principle names are Zinsser's.
